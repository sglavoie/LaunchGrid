Support

To report a problem or ask a question, open an issue on GitHub. You'll need a free GitHub account. Issues are read by the developer, Sébastien Lavoie, and usually answered within a few days.

Issues are public, so leave out anything personal. To help find the cause quickly, include:

  • Your macOS version (Apple menu › About This Mac).
  • Your LaunchGrid version (LaunchGrid › About LaunchGrid, with Settings open).
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot often helps.

You can also look through existing issues in case your question has been answered.

Common questions

The shortcut doesn't open LaunchGrid

Another app may already use the same shortcut. Open Settings › General and record a new one; LaunchGrid warns you when a shortcut is taken. You can always open LaunchGrid by clicking its icon in the menu bar or the Dock.

Where are Settings?

Right-click the LaunchGrid icon in the menu bar and choose Settings, or press ⌘ , while the grid is showing.

An app is missing from the grid

Choose Add Missing Apps from the menu-bar icon's menu. It lists installed apps that aren't on the grid, including ones you removed earlier. If the app lives outside your Applications folder, add its folder in Settings › Library, or use Browse to add the app itself.

I removed an app by mistake

Press ⌘ Z to undo, or bring it back with Add Missing Apps. Removing an app from the grid never deletes it from your Mac.

How do I make a folder?

Drag one app onto another and hold it there for a moment. To rename the folder, open it and click its title. A folder disappears when its last app is taken out.

Can I start from my old Launchpad layout?

If your Mac still has the Launchpad database from macOS 15 or earlier, the welcome window offers to import it. You can also import later from Settings › Library. LaunchGrid only reads the database; it never changes it.

How do I move my layout to another Mac?

In Settings › Library, choose Save Grid File. On the other Mac, double-click the saved file, or choose Load Grid File.

Open at Login is unavailable

macOS only allows this for apps in the Applications folder. Move LaunchGrid there and open it again.

How do I start over?

Choose Rebuild from Scratch in the menu-bar icon's menu to lay out your apps again by category or alphabetically. Undo brings back the old layout if you change your mind.

How do I uninstall LaunchGrid?

Quit it from the menu-bar icon's menu, then move LaunchGrid from your Applications folder to the Trash. To also delete your layout and settings, delete the folders listed in the privacy policy.

Was LaunchGrid made with AI?

Yes. About 99% of its code, and of this website, was written by Claude, Anthropic's AI model, under Sébastien Lavoie's direction. The app itself contains no AI and connects to nothing. The home page explains how it was made.

Privacy

LaunchGrid collects no data and makes no network connections. The privacy policy has the details.
