Privacy policy

Effective October 21, 2026

In short: LaunchGrid collects no data about you. It makes no network connections, so nothing it reads or stores ever leaves your Mac. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

This policy covers the LaunchGrid app for macOS and this website. LaunchGrid is made by Sébastien Lavoie, who is responsible for both. Questions about this policy can be sent as described in Contact.

Data the app collects

None. LaunchGrid has no code that sends information anywhere: no analytics or crash-reporting service, no advertising, no third-party code of any kind, and no connection to a server run by the developer or anyone else. The developer cannot see who uses LaunchGrid or how.

What the app reads on your Mac

To show your apps, LaunchGrid reads, on your Mac only:

  • The applications in your Applications folders, and in any other folder you add in Settings: their names, icons, bundle identifiers and locations on disk. It watches those folders so that apps you install or delete appear on or leave the grid.
  • Your desktop picture, if you choose it as the grid's background, or the picture you pick yourself.
  • A layout file, or an older layout database, but only when you choose one to import or load.
  • In the copy downloaded from GitHub, your Mac's keyboard shortcut settings, to warn you when the shortcut you pick for LaunchGrid is already used by macOS. The App Store version doesn't read them.

It does not read your documents, the contents of other apps, your contacts, your location, or anything you type outside its own window.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your layout: pages, folders, their names and order, and the apps you removed from the grid, so they aren't added again.
  • Your settings: the keyboard shortcut, appearance, grid size, background and similar choices.

Both stay on your Mac. The App Store version keeps them in its own folder, ~/Library/Containers/com.sglavoie.launchgrid. A copy downloaded from GitHub or built from the source code keeps the layout in ~/Library/Application Support/LaunchGrid and the settings in ~/Library/Preferences/com.sglavoie.launchgrid.plist. Files you save with Save Grid File go where you choose to save them.

If you back up your Mac with Time Machine or another backup tool, these files are backed up along with the rest of your Library, under your control.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your layout and settings are kept until you delete them. To delete everything LaunchGrid has stored, quit LaunchGrid, delete the app, and delete the folders named above. Rebuild from Scratch, in the menu-bar icon's menu, or the Rebuild buttons in Settings › Library, replace the layout without deleting the app.

Because nothing is collected, there is no copy held by the developer to request, correct or delete, and no consent to withdraw beyond deleting the files yourself.

Permissions

LaunchGrid's global shortcut does not need Accessibility or Input Monitoring access. If you turn on Open at Login, macOS lists LaunchGrid in System Settings › General › Login Items, where you can turn it off again.

Information Apple and GitHub share

If you download LaunchGrid from the Mac App Store, Apple handles the download and may provide the developer with aggregate figures, such as the number of downloads by country. If you agree in macOS to share crash reports with app developers, Apple may pass along reports of LaunchGrid's crashes, which describe the app's state when it stopped and do not identify you. Apple's handling of this information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

If you download LaunchGrid from GitHub, GitHub handles the download and shows the developer only how many times each release was downloaded, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. It is served by Cloudflare, which processes visitors' IP addresses and request details to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse, as described in Cloudflare's privacy policy. The developer does not use this information to identify visitors.

The support page links to GitHub. If you open an issue there, what you write is public and GitHub's privacy statement applies. Please don't post anything personal you don't want to share.

Children

LaunchGrid collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If LaunchGrid ever starts to handle data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. Previous versions are kept in the website's source history.

Contact

For questions about this policy or about LaunchGrid's handling of data, open an issue on GitHub, or see the support page.
